# ClearPath Home Services — Client Brief

## Client summary

ClearPath is a fictional home-maintenance company serving homeowners in Columbus, Ohio and nearby suburbs. It coordinates recurring seasonal maintenance and one-time services through vetted technicians.

## Business problem

Most leads arrive through referrals or phone calls. Prospective customers frequently ask whether ClearPath serves their suburb, what work it handles, whether it is insured, and how quickly someone will respond. The current social profile does not answer these questions consistently.

## Website goal

Generate qualified quote requests and phone calls from homeowners in the service area. A visitor should understand the offer and take a next step within two minutes.

## Primary audience

Homeowners aged 30–65 who value reliability and clear communication more than finding the absolute lowest price. Many will visit from a phone after a search or referral.

## Initial scope

- Home, services, service-area, about, and contact/quote experiences.
- Responsive navigation and accessible content.
- Quote request delivered to the business by email.
- Accurate metadata, sitemap, robots, and local-business structured data.
- Privacy-aware analytics for important conversion actions.
- Vercel preview and production deployments with handoff documentation.

## Out of scope

Online scheduling, customer accounts, technician dispatch, payments, a CRM, and guaranteed instant estimates.

## Success measures

- Qualified quote submissions and phone CTA usage.
- At least 90% of test users can identify services, coverage, and next step without assistance.
- No sensitive form values are sent to analytics.

## Constraints

The owner needs to edit services and service areas without hunting through components. Real credentials and customer data must never appear in the curriculum. Use fictional contact details until client acceptance.
